# Product Discovery Coach

Coach a learner through product discovery and give feedback on their practice answers.

## When to use
Use when the user is learning product discovery or wants feedback on a discovery artifact (problem framing, assumptions, tests).

## Inputs
- The lesson/topic (framing the problem, knowing users, riskiest assumption, testing cheaply, deciding on evidence).
- The learner's short practice answer.

## How it works
Walks a stepper of discovery lessons; for each practice answer it retrieves relevant PM skills from the shared Pro-PM library and returns encouraging, specific feedback: one thing right, one improvement, and a better version to copy.

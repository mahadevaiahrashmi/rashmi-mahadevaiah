Transparency & accountability tools to build

1. Protest & request tracker

A public board of live protests and citizen requests. For each one, show which elected official is responsible and define clear KPIs to measure whether the demand was actually delivered once it's granted — not just promised.

2. Government-school grievance portal

Students submit grievances that get routed — like an IT support ticket — straight to the government officer in charge and their elected representative, with a visible counter for how long each issue has been pending and where it's stuck.

3. Open-source accountability integrations

Bring existing civic-tech trackers under one roof, each issue linked to the responsible elected official and a live days-pending counter.

Inspiration: blr-potholes  ·  rasthe.in

Policy proposals demands

4. No impunity through delay — sexual-assault cases against MPs & MLAs

Sitting MPs and MLAs facing rape charges should have their cases heard on a strict, time-bound fast-track basis, so that power and endless procedural delay can't be used to escape justice.

Original wording: processed by the Supreme Court within 30 days. Worth refining — India already has SC-mandated special fast-track courts for cases against sitting legislators; the fight is to fund and actually enforce them. (Also: charged/on-trial, not merely accused.) Let's debate the right number in the community.

5. Roads that last — mandatory quality standards

Make durable materials and a standardized, audited build process mandatory for all public roads — with real accountability (and warranty/defect-liability periods) when a road fails early.

Cited example: Jangli Maharaj Road, Pune. Stronger than "copy one road" is enforcing material specs + contractor liability periods + independent quality audits.

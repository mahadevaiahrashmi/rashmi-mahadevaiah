# GTM with AI Videos

Turn a product launch into a short-form AI-video go-to-market plan to land the first 10 users.

## When to use
Use when the user is launching something and wants a concrete, video-first GTM plan for Instagram / LinkedIn.

## Inputs
- What's launching (product + audience).
- Target channel and angle.

## How it works
Produces 3-5 video concepts with paste-ready Higgsfield / Google Veo prompts, scripts, captions, a ~2-week posting schedule, and a channel playbook to convert viewers into the first 10 users. Optional Veo generation renders the videos (needs Google billing).
